## Geo pricing — what the research showed

Three distinct patterns the schema must capture:
1. **Currency localisation at checkout** — Gemini, Canva, Toggl (EUR), Plaky, Zoho, tl;dv (IP-based, no selector).
2. **Cheaper regional tiers in emerging markets** — Gemini AI Plus ($5–8 in 40+ countries).
3. **Availability / compliance constraints** — EU data hosting (tl;dv yes; Linear & Teamwork optional; Fireflies/Asana enterprise-only; Trello/Basecamp none); affiliate programs restricted by affiliate country (Lindy US/UK/CA/AU).

Trustpilot caveat: consumer AI brands score 1.2–1.6 regardless of product quality (billing/cancellation complaints). Show Trustpilot with its count; use G2/Capterra as the primary external signal.
